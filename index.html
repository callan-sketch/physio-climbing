THE CURRICULUM

Six modules, start to finish

Each module unlocks once you pass the quiz for the one before it — work through it at your own pace.

01

Introduction to Climbing

The sport itself: disciplines, competition formats (Speed, Lead, Boulder), and the vocabulary you'll need for every module after this one.

02

Anatomy of a Climber's Hand

Full osteology, the flexor pulley system, grip biomechanics, and a dedicated section on growth plates and youth-specific injury.

03

Common Climbing Injuries & Mechanics

Injury prevalence by body region and discipline, and how acute versus overuse presentations differ mechanically.

04

Assessing & Diagnosing Common Climbing Injuries

A site-by-site framework — fingers, wrist, elbow, shoulder, knee, and ankle — with differential tables for every presentation.

05

Treating & Rehabilitating Climbing Injuries

Evidence-based loading progressions, climbing-specific exercise selection, and grip-type-aware reloading after injury.

06

Return to Sport

Grading readiness by discipline, load progression, protective taping, working with coaches, and monitoring for re-injury.
